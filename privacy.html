Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StudentX ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the StudentX app and website. By creating an account, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect
  • Account information: full name, username, email, university, department, and any profile photo or cover photo you upload.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, videos, stories, messages, group and community activity, and StudyHub resources.
  • Usage data: likes, saves, follows, watch time, and interests, used to personalize your feed and video recommendations.
  • Device and log data: basic technical information such as browser type, used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Payment-related data: for Premium subscriptions, ads, and withdrawals, limited to what is required to process the transaction.
How We Use Your Information
  • To operate core features: your feed, chat, StudyHub, groups, live streams, and notifications.
  • To personalize content, including the videos and ads you see, based on your activity and interests.
  • To keep StudentX safe, including detecting spam, abuse, and violations of our community guidelines.
  • To communicate with you about your account, support requests, and important updates.
  • To improve the app through aggregated, non-identifying analysis of how features are used.
What Other People Can See

Your name, username, profile photo, and public posts are visible to other users according to the privacy setting you choose (Public, Followers only, or Private). Direct messages are only visible to you and the people in that conversation.

Third-Party Services
  • Supabase: our database, authentication, and file storage provider, which stores your account and content data securely.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to log in with Google, we receive only the basic profile information needed to create your account.
  • Advertising partners: sponsored posts and ads on StudentX may be delivered through advertising services; we do not sell your personal messages or private data to advertisers.
Your Rights and Choices
  • You can review and edit your profile information at any time from your Profile page.
  • You can set your account or individual posts to Followers-only or Private in Settings.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting support through Help & Support.
  • You can block or report any account that violates our community guidelines.
Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Children's Privacy

StudentX is intended for students of secondary school age and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable minimum age in their country without appropriate consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app before they take effect.
