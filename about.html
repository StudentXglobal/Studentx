About StudentX

StudentX is a modern social and academic platform designed specifically for students. It connects peers, empowers study groups, and provides an engaging environment for sharing knowledge, notes, and academic updates.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between social interaction and academic success, making learning collaborative, accessible, and enjoyable for students everywhere.
